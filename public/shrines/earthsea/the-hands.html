The Graphic Novel

Yeah A Wizard of Earthsea is getting a graphic novel adaptation in March 2025. I'm excited. It looks really cool from the cover reveal we got and I like the illustrators art style.

I feel that this is very safe adaptation wise. you are directly taking the dialogue from the book and adding pictures to it. sure some things may be edited slightly but unless theyre going batshit crazy this seems like a slam dunk to me.

my only real concern is that a lot of earthsea is internal thoughts feelings and struggles which would be difficult to adapt to a visual medium.
